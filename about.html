DISCOVER AAROHAN

What is AAROHAN?

AAROHAN stands for Advanced AI Readiness & Opportunity Hub for Aspirational Navigators.

The AAROHAN AI & Digital Skills Fellowship is designed to help young people build the knowledge, skills and confidence required to participate in today's technology-driven world.

The programme focuses on Artificial Intelligence, Digital Skills, Problem Solving, English Communication and Emerging Technology Careers.

AAROHAN encourages participants to move beyond theoretical learning and explore practical ways of applying technology to real-world challenges.

THE PURPOSE

Why AAROHAN?

Technology is changing the way people learn, work, communicate and solve problems. Artificial Intelligence and digital skills are becoming important parts of modern careers.

AAROHAN provides an environment where young learners can discover their potential, develop practical skills and become more confident in using technology.

Build Artificial Intelligence awareness
Develop practical digital skills
Improve problem-solving abilities
Strengthen English communication
Explore emerging technology careers
OUR VISION

Creating a Future-Ready Generation

Our vision is to empower young people with the skills, confidence and opportunities needed to participate meaningfully in the digital future.

We envision a generation that can use Artificial Intelligence and emerging technologies not only for employment, but also to solve real problems, create opportunities and contribute to their communities.
