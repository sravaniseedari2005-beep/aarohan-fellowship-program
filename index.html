Building Future-Ready Youth

AAROHAN is an AI and Digital Skills Fellowship programme focused on identifying and nurturing young talent through practical training and technology-focused learning.

The programme combines Artificial Intelligence, digital skills, problem solving, communication skills and emerging technologies to help participants become ready for future career opportunities.

Eligibility Criteria

Location

Age

Applicants between 18–28 years.

Education

Intermediate / ITI / Diploma / Degree / Post-Graduation.

Computer Skills

Basic computer knowledge is desirable.

HOW IT WORKS

Selection Process

01

Screening Test

Candidates participate in a screening test designed to assess their aptitude and learning potential.

02

Personal Interview

Shortlisted candidates participate in a personal interview as part of the selection process.

Screening Test Areas

  • Logical Reasoning
  • Basic Mathematics
  • English Comprehension
  • Digital Aptitude
  • Problem Solving
  • Learning Potential
